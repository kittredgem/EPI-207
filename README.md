## EPI 207: Assignment 2
Project description: Our study utilizes 2025 CHIS data to study the relationship between the exposure of having visited the doctor in the past 12 months on the outcome of having been to the ER in the past 12 months. This study is to complete assignment 2 in EPI 207.

## Features
With 2025 CHIS data having only been released in the past few weeks, this study provides research on a novel data set.
